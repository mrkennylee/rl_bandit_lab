🔗 A bandit is a one-state MDP

The two labs teach the same idea at different scales. A Gridworld is a full Markov Decision Process — many states, stochastic transitions, and the Bellman optimality equation. A multi-armed bandit is the special case with a single state and no discounting (γ = 0), so the Bellman equation collapses into the action-value update the bandit was learning.
